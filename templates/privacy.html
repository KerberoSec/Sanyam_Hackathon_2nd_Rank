Data Protection & Sovereignty

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 · HabitFlow v3.0.3

Zero Data Selling
Your habits and personal notes are never monetized, traded, or brokered.
Salted Hashing
Passwords are hashed using industry-standard Scrypt encryption algorithms.
Local Behavioral AI
AI coaching runs on your local server. No habit or mood data is sent to external LLMs.
Right to Erase
Permanently purge individual habits, account history, or records upon request.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver accurate streak calculations and individualized habit tracking, HabitFlow collects the minimum necessary data:

  • Account Credentials: Display name, email address, and a cryptographically salted password hash. We never store plain text passwords.
  • Habit Definitions: Titles, categories, icons, color selections, weekly schedules (e.g. Monday-Friday), and optional notification reminder times.
  • Daily Activity Logs: Status records ('completed', 'skipped', 'missed') attached to UTC calendar dates, along with earned gamification experience points.
  • Emotional Wellbeing Records: Self-reported daily mood check-ins ('happy', 'neutral', 'sad') and optional personal reflection notes (up to 1,000 characters).
  • Gamification State: Cumulative XP points, current player level, and unlocked milestone badges with achievement timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information logged in HabitFlow is processed exclusively to provide personal productivity metrics:

  • Computing unbroken streak counters, longest streaks, consistency percentages, and 12-month completion trends.
  • Detecting multi-day inactivity and offering supportive comeback guidance.
  • Analyzing statistical correlations between your self-reported emotional states and habit adherence.
  • Securing authenticated sessions via cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and enforcing distributed rate limits.

3. Local-First Behavioral AI & Coaching

HabitFlow's AI Coach operates using an internal, rule-based algorithmic behavioral engine. All habit suggestions, weekly summary reviews, and mood correlations are generated locally on the host application server. We do not transmit your habit history, notes, or mood data to third-party artificial intelligence providers, external APIs, or commercial LLM services.

4. Security Architecture & Safeguards

HabitFlow implements rigorous, production-grade security safeguards:

  • Password Hashing: Werkzeug / Scrypt key-derivation algorithms with unique cryptographic salts.
  • Token Revocation: Each user record maintains an incrementing token_version. Changing passwords or logging out immediately revokes all prior JWT tokens server-side.
  • Modern HTTP Headers: Strict Content-Security-Policy (CSP), X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and restrictive Permissions-Policy.
  • Anti-Abuse Protections: Shared database rate limiters prevent brute-force attacks across login and password recovery endpoints.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

HabitFlow uses localStorage solely to maintain your authentication bearer token, basic profile cache (name, level, XP), and your dark mode visual theme preference. We do not use third-party analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Soft vs. Hard Deletion: Archiving a habit preserves past completion history for analytics. Calling delete with ?permanent=true completely purges the habit and all attached daily logs.
  • Account Erasure: Deleting an account executes a cascade deletion across all associated habits, logs, mood check-ins, messages, and achievement badges.

7. Contact & Security Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to report a security disclosure, please contact developer Arun Kumar via GitHub at @KerberoSec.
