About HabitFlow

Empowering Daily Growth Through Consistency

HabitFlow is an intelligent personal habit tracker engineered to help you build resilient daily routines, track emotional wellbeing, and celebrate incremental progress through thoughtful gamification.

Get Started Free View Dashboard
Atomic Progress

Grand transformations start with micro-habits. We focus on low-friction actions that take less than five minutes to complete, making daily consistency effortless and sustainable.

Forgiving Streak Design

Real life isn't rigid. HabitFlow supports flexible weekly schedules, excused skip days that safeguard your streak, and morning grace periods so you never lose momentum unfairly.

Holistic Wellbeing

Your routines and emotions are deeply intertwined. Daily mood check-ins correlate how emotional states impact your productivity, unlocking meaningful self-awareness over 30 and 90 days.

Core Pillars of the HabitFlow Platform

Frequency-Aware Streak Engine

Unlike naive trackers that require logging every day, HabitFlow respects custom schedules (e.g. weekdays-only, MWF). Unscheduled days never break your active run.

Native Rule-Based Behavioral Coach

Our deterministic coaching service runs entirely locally on your server. It highlights milestone streaks, detects inactivity, notices power days, and suggests goal-matched micro-habits without third-party data leakage.

Engaging Gamification & Badges

Earn experience points (XP) for completions, excused skips, and 7/14/30-day streak milestones. Progress through dynamic levels and unlock achievement badges from 'First Week' to 'Yearly Champion'.

Security & Data Sovereignty

Built with modern defense-in-depth: Scrypt password hashing, stateful JWT revocation via token versions, strict Content Security Policies (CSP), and shared database rate limiters.

Open Source Engineering v3.0.3

Crafted with Precision by Arun Kumar

HabitFlow was architected to demonstrate enterprise-grade Flask application patterns: clean layered services, atomic streak calculations, idempotent gamification rewards, and cross-database compatibility across SQLite, PostgreSQL, and MySQL.
