Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental priorities at HabitFlow.

  • Account data: HabitFlow stores account, habit, mood, and progress data to provide your tracking history.
  • Password protection: Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never returned by the API.
  • Tracking: This application does not include third-party advertising pixels.
